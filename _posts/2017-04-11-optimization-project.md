Used geometric programming to minimize the volume of a foil (airfoil or hydrofoil) subjected to arbitrary loads and constraints including maximum deflection. The resulting convex optimization problem was solved using tools including MATLAB, the CVX modeling system, and Mosek 8.0 solver.
