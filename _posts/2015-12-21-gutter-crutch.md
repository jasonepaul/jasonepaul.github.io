Physiotherapist at Foothills Hospital Spinal Cord Center required a custom gutter crutch for patient mobility. AutoCAD was used for design work. Device was manufactured using a commercial gutter crutch, aluminum tubing, 3/4″ hardwood plywood, glue, and fasteners. Volunteers: Jason Paul, Adrian Black.
