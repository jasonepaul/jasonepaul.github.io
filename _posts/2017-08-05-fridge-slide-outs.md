A “little person” with limited mobility needed a custom solution to enable access to items in her refrigerator. Existing shelves were replaced with fabricated full extension slide-out ones. AutoCAD was used for design work. Materials include acrylic, maple wood, full extension drawer slides, fasteners, wood glue, and plastic welding solvent. Volunteer: Jason Paul.
