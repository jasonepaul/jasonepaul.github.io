Client required a custom holder for large cups to attach to her wheelchair. The solution was to obtain a basic large commercial folding cup holder and secure it to a bracket fabricated out of aluminum. The devise is then placed over the armrest on the wheelchair. AutoCAD was used to assist design. Volunteer: Jason Paul.
